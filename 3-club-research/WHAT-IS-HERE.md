# Research notes: what is in this folder

What is already known about RoboMaster's public sources. Your AI reads these first, before searching online.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `sources-playbook.md` | **The map:** for each kind of question, which sites to use, which ones block AIs, and the way around. |
| `2026-09-08-rm-bbs-source-index.json` | An index of every RoboMaster forum article (title, date, link, topic). It is not shipped: your AI builds it with `bbs-index` (about 15 requests, under a minute) and refreshes it when it is more than 30 days old. |
| anything else | Research notes your AI saves here as it answers your questions, so the next question starts from them. |

The playbook was checked on the dates written inside it. Sources change, so your AI re-checks anything important.
