# Your AI's toolbox (you can ignore this folder)

Your AI uses everything here; you never need to open it.

| What | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `skills/` | Step-by-step instructions for setup, research, video transcription and course notes. |
| `tools/` | The programs your AI runs. |
| `requirements.txt` | What setup installs. |
| `.venv/` | Appears after setup: the installed tools. |
| `working-files/` | Appears when you make a course: transcripts and progress. Keep them on your computer. |
| `kit-layout.json` | Tells the tools where courses go. |

To start over, delete `.venv/` and ask your AI to "set up the research kit" again.
